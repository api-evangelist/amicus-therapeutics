Amicus Therapeutics is a global rare disease biotechnology company focused on discovering, developing, and commercializing medicines for people living with genetically defined conditions. Founded on February 4, 2002 and headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, Amicus markets two approved therapies: Galafold (migalastat) for adults with Fabry disease who have an amenable GLA variant, and the two-component Pombiliti (cipaglucosidase alfa-atga) + Opfolda (miglustat) regimen for adults with late-onset Pompe disease.

BioMarin Pharmaceutical agreed to acquire Amicus in December 2025 for $4.8 billion and completed the acquisition on April 27, 2026. Amicus now operates as a wholly owned subsidiary of BioMarin; the NASDAQ ticker FOLD has been delisted.

- **Website:** [https://www.amicusrx.com](https://www.amicusrx.com)
- **Parent company:** [BioMarin Pharmaceutical](https://www.biomarin.com)
- **Investor relations (BioMarin):** [https://investors.biomarin.com/](https://investors.biomarin.com/)
- **Headquarters:** Princeton, New Jersey
